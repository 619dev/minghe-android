Minghe is an Android client for self-hosted Minghe SIP servers. Enter the server hostname, your extension, and your SIP password to get online.

Make and receive audio calls over SIP/TLS. Select SDES-SRTP or ZRTP according to your server configuration. In ZRTP calls, compare the authentication code with your peer.

An ongoing Android notification accompanies background registration.

Available in Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, Spanish, and German.
